Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Total Automotive 2013 Ltd respects your privacy and follows the Privacy Act 2020. This explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

This website has no forms and doesn't ask you for personal details. If you call or email us, we keep what you choose to tell us, such as your name, phone number, email address and vehicle details, so we can answer your enquiry and look after your vehicle.

How we use it

We use your details to quote, book and carry out work on your vehicle, and to contact you about it. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone, except where we need to for the job (for example a parts supplier) or where the law requires it.

Other websites

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the home page shows a Google map, so your browser connects to Google while you browse. Links on this site open Google Maps, Google reviews, Gmail, Facebook and Battery Town. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you at any time. Call us on 03 208 5222 or email totalautomotive2013@yahoo.co.nz.
