FAQ

Good to know before you book

What vehicles do you work on?

Cars, utes, vans and 4WDs, plus the odd classic or American import. If you're not sure, give us a ring on 03 208 5222.

Do you do auto electrical work too?

Yes. Mechanical and auto electrical repairs are both done in our workshop, from batteries, alternators and starter motors to wiring faults and air conditioning.

Can you sort my vehicle after a failed WOF?

Yes. Bring in your WOF check sheet and we'll quote and fix what's needed, so you're ready for the recheck.

Do you sell batteries?

Yes. We stock Battery Town batteries for cars, utes, tractors and machinery, plus deep cycle batteries for boats and campers.

How do I book my vehicle in?

Call us on 03 208 5222 or email us, Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. December books up fast, so get in early if you need your vehicle sorted before the holidays.

Where are you?

We're at 10 Waiau Street, Gore 9710, with easy parking out front. Get directions.
